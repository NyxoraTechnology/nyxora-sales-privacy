<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="muted">App: Nyxora Sales (package: com.nyxora.sales)<br>
Operated by: Nyxora Technology<br>
Last updated: 31 October 2026</p>

<p>This Privacy Policy explains how the Nyxora Sales mobile app ("the App") collects, uses, stores and shares information. The App is a business tool for employees and authorised team members of Nyxora Technology and its clients. It is not intended for the general public or for children under 18.</p>

<h2>1. Information we collect</h2>
<table>
<tr><th>Data</th><th>Why we collect it</th></tr>
<tr><td><b>Account information</b>: name, work email, phone number, employee ID, role, and password (stored in hashed form on our servers)</td><td>To create your account, sign you in and show you the correct data for your role.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Device information</b>: device identifier, model, OS version, and a push notification token</td><td>To verify and bind your device to your account, prevent unauthorised access, and send notifications.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Location</b> (precise GPS, only while you use attendance or field visit features)</td><td>To record attendance within the allowed work area (geofence) and to record the location of field visits.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Camera / face image</b></td><td>To verify your identity when you mark attendance, and to attach photos to visits where you choose to.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Call logs and phone state</b> (numbers, call time, duration, call type) for calls made or received for business with leads and customers</td><td>To log business calls against leads, show call history, and calculate targets and incentives.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Microphone / call recordings</b> (where you or your organisation enable recording of business calls)</td><td>To store call recordings for quality, training and dispute resolution within your organisation.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Business data you enter</b>: leads, customers, notes, follow-ups, meetings, tasks, visits, documents, payments, leave and support requests</td><td>To provide the core sales and team management features of the App.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Files and photos</b> you choose to upload</td><td>To attach documents, proofs and images to the relevant records.</td></tr>
</table>

<h2>2. How we use the information</h2>
<ul>
<li>To provide and operate the App and its features (leads, calls, attendance, visits, tasks, reports, payroll and related modules).</li>
<li>To authenticate users and keep accounts and devices secure.</li>
<li>To send service notifications such as reminders, announcements and task updates.</li>
<li>To generate reports, targets and incentive calculations for your organisation.</li>
<li>To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, misuse and security incidents.</li>
<li>To comply with legal obligations.</li>
</ul>
<p>We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.</p>

<h2>3. Sharing of information</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Within your organisation:</b> managers and administrators authorised by your organisation can see attendance, visit, call and lead data relevant to their role.</li>
<li><b>Service providers:</b> we use trusted providers for cloud hosting, data storage and push notifications (for example Google Firebase Cloud Messaging). They process data only on our instructions.</li>
<li><b>Legal reasons:</b> we may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.</li>
</ul>

<h2>4. Data storage and security</h2>
<p>Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on our servers. Access tokens are stored in secure storage on your device. We use access controls and reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no system is completely secure.</p>

<h2>5. Data retention and deletion</h2>
<p>We keep data for as long as your account is active and as needed for business, legal and audit purposes. You can request correction or deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us at the address below. Some records may be kept where we are required by law or for legitimate business records.</p>

<h2>6. Permissions used by the App</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>Location:</b> attendance and field visits.</li>
<li><b>Camera:</b> face verification and photo capture.</li>
<li><b>Microphone:</b> call recording and voice features, where enabled.</li>
<li><b>Phone and call log:</b> place calls from the App, detect business calls and log them against leads.</li>
<li><b>Notifications:</b> reminders and alerts.</li>
</ul>
<p>You can change permissions at any time in your device settings, but some features will not work without them.</p>

<h2>7. Children</h2>
<p>The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.</p>

<h2>8. Changes to this policy</h2>
<p>We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date changed.</p>
